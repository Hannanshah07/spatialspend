03

Cognitive Science: Spatial Anchoring

Episodic Memory

Humans reconstruct personal past events through spatio-temporal cues: walking past the fountain, sunlight angle, and neighboring landmarks.

Commute Corridors

68% of weekday card micro-taps occur within 200m of the cardholder's regular transit artery (e.g. Market St / Powell BART).

Zero Verification Stress

By presenting a map pin instead of a legal string, recognition latency drops from 46.5 seconds down to 8.2 seconds.

Pain Points & Blindspots

  • Taps coffee carts and pastry windows where parent LLC names are totally unrecognizable.
  • Frequently pauses or locks cards believing someone is making $6 fraudulent charges.
  • Spends over 30 minutes at end-of-month reconciliations sorting out expense receipts.

SpatialSpend Needs

  • Single tap to see the exact street corner where contactless NFC was authorized.
  • Quick memory cue (e.g. "Purchased 8 mins after exiting Caltrain").
  • Automatic separation of online takeout orders from physical store visits.

Scale & Micro-Typography

Pin typography prioritizes high-contrast font weight (Plus Jakarta Sans 800) with a dark halo drop-shadow, making amounts legible over dark asphalt street tiles.

Cartographic Suppression

Default map noise (gas stations, irrelevant business labels, building heights) was completely stripped to make financial transaction pins the dominant visual layer.

06

Edge Cases & Ethical Privacy Guards

E-Commerce Segregation

Online orders (Amazon, Netflix, Uber Eats) do not generate false pins on server farms. They are isolated into a dedicated floating "Digital & Subscriptions" drawer.

Subterranean Transit Taps

Subway stations have zero GPS reception. Underground NFC turns immediately snap to the station turnstile entrance node rather than drifting wildly.

Home Privacy Fuzzing

Transactions occurring within 250m of a cardholder's home address are given a diffuse boundary circle to prevent leaking residential locations in monthly export PDFs.
